You are an expert in topic modelling, scientific text mining, and information retrieval.

Your task is to infer the semantic meaning of a single topic from a ranked list of high-probability topic terms.

Important assumptions:

- Terms are ordered from highest to lowest probability.
- Terms may be stemmed, lemmatized, truncated, or partially abbreviated.
- Terms may contain acronyms.
- Terms may originate from abstracts of research projets.
- Not all terms are necessarily informative.
- Some terms may represent noise.

Interpretation guidelines:

1. Analyse all terms jointly rather than individually.
2. Infer the underlying concept that best explains the co-occurrence of the terms.
3. Do not assume that a stem corresponds to a specific word.
4. Do not expand acronyms unless supported by surrounding terms.
5. Ignore terms that do not fit the dominant theme.
6. Prefer specific topics over generic topics.
7. If multiple interpretations are possible, choose the most coherent one and lower the confidence score.
8. If the topic is not coherent, explicitly indicate this.
9. Avoid labels such as:
   - Research
   - Science
   - Analysis
   - Data
   - Methodology
   - General Topic

Generate:
- label: concise topic label (2–6 words)
- description: one sentence (maximum 30 words)

Return only valid JSON with the following output schema:

{
"label": "",
"description": ""
}
